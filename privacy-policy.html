Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Rehnuma-E-Munshiyaan mobile application (“the App”) handles information. The App is an independent educational digital book reader for court documents and procedures. It is not a government application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorized by the Government of Pakistan or any court or ministry.

1. Information we collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, or location.

The following data may be stored only on your device:

  • Last reading page / progress
  • Bookmarks
  • Local reading history

This data stays on your phone and is not uploaded to our servers.

2. How we use information

Local reading data is used only to:

  • Remember where you left off
  • Show your bookmarks and history inside the App

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

3. Internet & third-party services

Book content is bundled with the App for offline reading. The App may use the internet only for optional features such as loading fonts (for example via Google Fonts). No reading progress or bookmarks are sent over the network by the App.

4. Children’s privacy

The App is a general educational / professional reference tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data retention & deletion

Because data is stored only on your device, you can clear it at any time by clearing the App’s storage/cache in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.

7. Disclaimer & official sources

The App is based on an independent educational book. Copyright registration of the literary work (IPO Pakistan, Registration No. 44654-Copr.) relates only to the book and does not imply government affiliation or endorsement of the App. Users should verify current laws and procedures from official sources such as:

  • Government of Pakistan
  • Ministry of Law and Justice
  • Supreme Court of Pakistan
  • Lahore High Court
  • Islamabad High Court
